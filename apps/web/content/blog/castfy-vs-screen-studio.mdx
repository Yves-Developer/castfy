Screen Studio and Castfy both turn a screen recording into a polished demo, with zoom, a smooth cursor and a nice background. The difference is who does the recording: with Screen Studio you record your screen yourself, on a Mac; with Castfy your coding agent clicks through the app for you, on Mac, Windows or Linux. Pick Screen Studio if you want to record yourself with full creative control; pick Castfy if you'd rather describe the demo in a sentence and let an agent make it.

## Where Screen Studio is better

- **You control every frame.** You decide exactly where the mouse goes and when. For a launch video or a hero clip you'll obsess over, that matters.
- **Your face and your voice.** Webcam layouts, mic noise reduction and captions from your own speech make it a strong tool for talking-head walkthroughs and tutorials.
- **More editing and export options.** Edit by transcript, cut filler words, background music, 4K at 60 fps, GIFs and hosted share links.
- **Any app, not just the browser.** It records anything on your Mac screen, including native apps and iPhone or iPad over USB.

## Where Castfy is better

- **You don't record anything.** Paste a URL, say what to show in a sentence, and your coding agent clicks through the flow, even past a sign-in. No rehearsing, no fumbled takes.

- **Windows and Linux too.** Screen Studio is made for Mac and nothing else; Castfy runs on all three.
- **Re-record when your UI changes.** When a button moves, ask for a new take instead of recording the demo again by hand. Point at the step that changed with @.
- **The voice comes with the demo.** The agent writes a line for each step from what actually happened on screen, and the video waits for every word. With Screen Studio's AI voiceover you type the script yourself.
- **Pay once.** Castfy is a one-time purchase. Screen Studio is a subscription; over two years on the yearly plan you'd pay about $216.

## Which one should you pick?

- **Pick Screen Studio** if you're on a Mac, you like recording yourself, and you want webcam, your own voice and fine editing.
- **Pick Castfy** if you'd rather not record at all, you're on Windows or Linux, you already use a coding agent like Claude Code, or your demo needs re-recording every time the product changes.

Some teams use both: Castfy for the many quick demos of each feature, Screen Studio for the one hero video they polish by hand.
